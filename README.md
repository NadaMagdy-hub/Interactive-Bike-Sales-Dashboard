# Interactive-Bike-Sales-Dashboard
# 🚴‍♂️ Interactive Retail Bike Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive and dynamic data analysis dashboard created in **Microsoft Excel** using **Power Query**, **Power Pivot**, and **DAX** to analyze 3 months of global bike sales performance across 6 countries, 32 states, and 60 bike models.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

![Dashboard Preview](your-dashboard-image-or-gif-link-here.png)

> 💡 **Project Goal:** To transform raw sales data into actionable commercial insights, identify high-potential expansion markets, and uncover inventory & regional distribution bottlenecks.

---

## 📈 Key Financial Metrics & KPIs

* **Total Revenue:** `$1.41M`
* **Net Profit:** `$513.4K`
* **Profit Margin:** `36.43%` *(Exceeds industry standard of 25%–35%)*
* **Total Orders:** `752`

---

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

1. **Core Revenue Drivers:**
   * **Road Bikes** drive **73%** of total sales (`$1M+`).
   * The **Young Adults (23–35)** age group is the top demographic, driving over **$642K** in sales.

2. **Canada Expansion Potential:**
   * Canada appears as a low-revenue market overall, but deep-dive analysis reveals it has only **ONE** recorded state (*British Columbia*), which ranks **7th highest globally**. This highlights a high-demand market ready for regional expansion.

3. **France Distribution Inequality:**
   * France is among the top 3 revenue-generating countries, yet **12 out of its 15 states** rank in the bottom 15 globally, indicating a severe sales concentration bottleneck.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Methods

* **Power Query:** Data cleaning, type transformation, deduplication, and schema standardisation.
* **Power Pivot & Data Modeling:** Star schema architecture, relationship building between data tables.
* **DAX Formulas:** Custom measures for dynamic aggregations (`Total Revenue`, `Total Cost`, `Profit Margin %`, `SWITCH()` age binning).
* **Excel Dashboard UX:** Multi-slicer connection, cross-filtering, and dynamic charting.
